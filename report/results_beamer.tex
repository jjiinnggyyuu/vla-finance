\section{Summary}
\begin{frame}{Key takeaways}\large
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Normalisation works}: halves regime bias (Long\_ns std $0.30\!\to\!0.18$) without multi-asset.
\item \textbf{Multi-asset (ETH+XRP) does not help} --- v15\_btc $>$ v15\_multi.
\item \textbf{Macro (bonds/S\&P) does not help} --- baseline ties/beats; under-powered.
\item \textbf{Kronos wins on true out-of-sample (2025)} --- our VLA does not yet beat the pretrained baseline.
\item Best single result: v15\_btc long-only @0.5\% $=+4.0\%$ (still $<$ B\&H).
\end{enumerate}\end{frame}
