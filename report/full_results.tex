\paragraph{Part A.1 --- what it says.} Over the full walk-forward, DA stays at $0.51$--$0.52$ for all variants. \textbf{v14} edges out on DA but is the most regime-biased (Long\_ns~$0.57$); \textbf{normalisation (v15\_btc) gives the most balanced Long\_ns ($\approx0.50$) and the only positive return (LO $+4.0\%$ at $k{=}6$).} Returns are strongly negative at $k{=}1$ (slippage from over-trading) and recover toward $k{=}6$--$12$. No variant beats B\&H ($+7.2\%$).
