\begin{frame}{Setup and metrics (read this once)}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Task.} Recent 10 hourly candles $\rightarrow$ next 12 candles (log-return deltas).
Backbone frozen; only the action head trains.\\[0.4em]
\textbf{Protocol.} Walk-forward 6-fold (2020--2025): train Jan--Oct, val Nov, \emph{test Dec}.
All metrics on BTC only, holding horizon $k{=}6$ hours.\\[0.6em]
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Metric & Meaning \\ \midrule
\textbf{DA} & directional accuracy (sign match), 0.5 = coin flip \\
\textbf{ret} & backtest return, \textbf{long-only} strategy (see below), 0.1\% slippage \\
\textbf{AER} & ret $-$ buy\&hold; \gd{$>0$ beats passive holding} \\
 & (B\&H = pure hold, start$\to$end price change; \textbf{$k$-independent}, ${+}7.1\%$ over folds) \\
$\boldsymbol{\sigma}\downarrow$ & regime index = across-fold std of long-ratio; \bd{high = bets one way per fold} \\
\textbf{MAE}$\downarrow$ & close-price error (USD); \bd{high = directionally lucky but numerically wrong} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\\[0.6em]
\textbf{Trading strategies} (signal $\hat r$ = predicted $k$-step return, gate $\theta$):\\
\quad\textbf{long-only}\;: $\hat r>\theta \Rightarrow$ long $k$\,h, else cash \hfill
\textbf{long-short}\;: $\hat r>\theta \Rightarrow$ long;\; $\hat r<-\theta \Rightarrow$ short; else cash\\
\quad$\theta$ picked per setup from $\{0.1,0.3,0.5,1.0\}\%$ by 6-fold-mean ret (one global $\theta$, not per fold).\\[0.4em]
\textbf{All main tables use long-only.} The Kronos slide shows both strategies, labeled.\\[0.4em]
\textbf{Four design axes:} (i)~assets, (ii)~head, (iii)~diffusion scope, (iv)~normalization.
\end{frame}

\begin{block}{Conclusion}

only MSAT's \emph{joint self-attention} exploits cross-asset structure; DiT's cross-attention just
gets distracted.
\end{block}

\end{block}

\begin{frame}{Verdict: the best from-scratch setup \hfill\normalfont\small($k{=}6$\,h, long-only)}
\footnotesize
\begin{columns}[T]
\column{0.5\textwidth}
\textbf{Best runs (by ret / AER):}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item \gd{DiT + BTC + norm} \\ DA 0.517, ret +0.040, AER $-$0.032
\item \gd{MSAT + multi + all-diff + norm} \\ DA 0.513, ret +0.027, AER $-$0.044
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Rules that hold:}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item[(iv)] \gd{normalize targets} --- always
\item[(i,ii)] match head to assets: \\ single$\to$DiT, multi$\to$MSAT \\
 {\scriptsize\bd{($k{=}6$ only --- see next slide}}
\item[(iii)] all-diff $\geq$ read-off
\end{itemize}

\column{0.5\textwidth}
\begin{block}{But the ceiling is hard}
Every from-scratch setup has \bd{AER $<0$}: on average \bd{nothing beats buy-and-hold}.\\[0.4em]
Across all four axes, structural choices only \emph{reallocate} a weak signal --- they do not
create one.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.3em}
\centering
\textit{The architecture is fine; the training is under-resourced.}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

the VLA design.}
\end{block}

% ============================================================
\begin{frame}{Engineering has plateaued --- new directions}
\footnotesize
Target-norm, head choice, asset mix, scope, input-norm: all tuned. \textbf{AER still $<0$.}
Engineering the pipeline reallocates signal but cannot manufacture it. Next, three
non-engineering bets:
\\[0.8em]
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep4pt
\item \textbf{Self-predicted action loss (13th token).} Generate 12 candles \emph{plus} a 13th
token trained to predict the model's own error $\Rightarrow$ built-in confidence signal.
\item \textbf{Confidence-gated trading.} Trade only when \emph{predicted loss is small and predicted
move is large} --- act on the model's high-conviction, high-payoff cases, sit out the rest.
\item \textbf{Financial pretraining of the VLA.} Give the VLA the advantage Kronos already has,
rather than training from scratch on 10 months per fold.
\end{enumerate}
